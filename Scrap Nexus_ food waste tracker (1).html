    Ways to keep food out of the trash

    Am I a “large food waste generator”?

    Under New Jersey’s Food Waste Recycling Law (P.L. 2020, c.24), businesses and institutions that make at least 52 tons of food waste a year must separate it and send it to an authorized recycling facility if one is within 25 road miles.

    Progress toward the 2030 goal

    New Jersey’s Food Waste Reduction Act (2017) sets a goal of cutting food waste 50% by 2030.
